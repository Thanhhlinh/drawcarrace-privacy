Privacy Policy – Draw Car Race

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Duong Thanh Linh ("we", "us") handles information when you play Draw Car Race (the "Game") on Android and iOS. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no account system. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information that directly identifies you.

Your game progress (cars, coins, gems, stages, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the following third-party services. They may automatically collect limited technical information from your device under their own privacy policies:

ServicePurposeData it may collect
Google AdMob Showing ads (including optional rewarded ads) Advertising ID, device model and OS, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad interactions, crash and performance diagnostics
AppsFlyer Measuring app installs, in-game events and ad revenue Advertising ID, device ID, device model and OS, IP address, in-game events, purchase and ad revenue events
Google Play Billing / Apple App Store Processing in-app purchases Purchase history. Payment details are handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never see your card information.

3. How the information is used

  • To show ads and measure their performance;
  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore them;
  • To understand how the Game is played and fix problems;
  • To measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Remove forced ads: the in-game "Remove Ads" purchase stops forced (interstitial) ads. Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch remain available.
  • Delete data: uninstalling the Game removes all game data stored on your device. To request deletion of data held by the services above, contact us at the email below with your device's Advertising ID and we will forward the request.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security

Data sent by the third-party services listed above is encrypted in transit.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
